# Pyrenees — folk music & other opportunities (26 Aug – 8 Sep 2026)

Companion to [[Pyrenees personal wants to go]]. Window checked against **today 18 Aug 2026**. Focus: folk / traditional music & dance near the Aragon–Navarra–Catalan Pyrenees route, plus extras that fit the same itinerary.

**How to use:** the standout folk fit is **Graus (EMTP)** at the start of the window; **La Seu d’Urgell** and **Vielha** cover traditional dance if the route swings east toward Taüll/Boí. Full activity detail (astronomy, sports) lives on the wants page.

## In the window

### ★ Encuentro Internacional de Músicas Tradicionales del Pirineo (EMTP) — Graus
- **When:** **26–30 Aug 2026** (11th edition — aggregators list these dates; 2025 ran 27–31 Aug).
- **Where:** Graus (Ribagorza, Huesca) — Plaza Mayor, Espacio Pirineos. **In-person.**
- **What:** European folk groups, free outdoor concerts, workshops on Pyrenean instruments, baile folk, pasacalles. Organised by Gaiteros de Graus.
- **Confirm:** [encuentrodemusicastradicionales.webnode.es](https://encuentrodemusicastradicionales.webnode.es/) · [gaiterosdegraus.webnode.es](https://gaiterosdegraus.webnode.es/) — line-up still **TENTATIVE**.

## Suggested routing

1. **26–30 Aug — Graus base:** EMTP folk days + day-trips to **Samitier** (sunset/drone), Loarre / Montearagón, maybe Barbastro approach.
2. **Optional swing west:** Valle de Tena sports + **26 Aug or 2 Sep** astronomy; Leyre / Javier / Isaba if time (Isaba’s own fiesta is later in Sep).
3. **28 Aug – 1 Sep — east option:** **La Seu** for Ball Cerdà (30 Aug), then **Boí / Taüll** hike (Senders Ribera de Sant Martí → Estany Gran del Pessó).
4. **5–8 Sep:** **Vielha Hèsta** and/or Durro opening if still in Vall de Boí / Aran.
