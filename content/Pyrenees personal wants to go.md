## Music


### ★ Encuentro Internacional de Músicas Tradicionales del Pirineo (EMTP) — Graus
- **When:** **26–30 Aug 2026** (11th edition — aggregators list these dates; 2025 ran 27–31 Aug).
- **Where:** Graus (Ribagorza, Huesca) — Plaza Mayor, Espacio Pirineos, heritage venues. **In-person.**
- **What:** European folk groups, free outdoor concerts, **workshops** on Pyrenean instruments (gaita de boto, chiflo, salterio, dulzaina…), **baile folk / talleres de baile**, pasacalles, sessions between musicians. Organised by Gaiteros de Graus.
- **Why it fits your list:** closest serious folk gathering to **Castillo de Samitier**, Loarre/Montearagón day-trips, and the road toward Benasque / Vall de Boí.
- **Cost:** concerts usually free; some workshops may need signup.
- **Confirm:** final 2026 cartel often drops late — check [encuentrodemusicastradicionales.webnode.es](https://encuentrodemusicastradicionales.webnode.es/) · [gaiterosdegraus.webnode.es](https://gaiterosdegraus.webnode.es/) (Sergi +34 625 116 533 / Guillermo +34 657 218 961) · listing: [defiestaenfiesta](https://www.defiestaenfiesta.es/eventos/aragon/huesca/graus/encuentro-internacional-de-musicas-tradicionales-del-pirineo-20260826).
- **Status:** dates widely published for 2026 but **line-up still TENTATIVE** — verify before locking travel.

### Era Hèsta de Vielha (Val d’Aran)
- **When:** **5–11 Sep 2026** (overlaps your window **5–8 Sep**; rest continues after).
- **Where:** Vielha historic centre. **In-person.**
- **What:** main Aranese summer festa — traditional **esquerrana** dances, evening concerts, artisan market, olla aranesa popular meals. Occitan/Aranese identity, not “festival folk” in the EMTP sense, but living mountain tradition.
- **Why it fits:** one valley over from **Boí / Taüll / Aigüestortes** (tunnel / N-230).
- **Programme:** usually late Aug on [visitvaldaran.com](https://www.visitvaldaran.com) · overview: [festivalesdeespana](https://www.festivalesdeespana.com/en/festival/era-hesta-de-vielha-vielha).

### Festa Major de Durro (Vall de Boí)
- **When:** **8–13 Sep 2026** (your window catches **Tue 8 Sep** opening: missa + vermut popular).
- **Where:** Durro — same valley as Boí / Erill / Taüll on your wants list.
- **Folk note:** traditional **Ball pla** is listed for **Sun 13 Sep** (just after your dates) with Ribatònics.
- **Source:** [surtdecasa.cat](https://surtdecasa.cat/pirineus/agenda/2026/festa-major-de-durro/267980) · valley context: [vallboi.cat](https://www.vallboi.cat/es/ideas-de-viaje/un-verano-cultural).
